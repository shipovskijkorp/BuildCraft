<chapter name="Quarry Frame"/>
A Quarry automatically places Frames around its selected area before mining begins. The frame shows the working boundary and supports the moving drill assembly.

Frames may also be used as inexpensive open scaffolding, but a Quarry manages and removes its own frame blocks as part of its work cycle.
