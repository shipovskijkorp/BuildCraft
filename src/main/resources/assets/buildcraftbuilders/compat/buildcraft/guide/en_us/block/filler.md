<chapter name="Area and Resources"/>
Place the Filler beside Land Marks or a Volume Box, then open its interface to refresh the work area. Supply building blocks to the resource slots and MJ to the internal battery. Excavating patterns can also remove existing blocks when excavation is enabled.

<chapter name="Patterns"/>
Available patterns include clear, fill, box, frame, horizon, flatten, cylinder, pyramid and stairs. Two-dimensional shapes include triangles, regular polygons, arcs and circles. Three-dimensional shapes include spheres and partial spheres.

Pattern parameters control direction, rotation, facing, axis, centre and hollow variants. Inverting the selection swaps the inside and outside of the generated pattern.
