<chapter name="Planning"/>
Attach the planner to a Volume Box and configure its pattern, parameters and inversion state. A Filler using that box can read the stored plan instead of being configured from scratch.

The preview follows the Volume Box, making it possible to resize and inspect the planned operation before committing materials and MJ.
