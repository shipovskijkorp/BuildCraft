<recipes_usages stack="buildcraftbuilders:architect"/>

<chapter name="Area Setup"/>
Place the table beside a connected Land Mark area or Volume Box. The selected box is read from the area provider behind or next to the table.

<chapter name="Scanning"/>
Insert a blank Blueprint to record exact blocks, states and supported data, or a blank Template to record only the shape. Choose whether the result may rotate, excavate existing blocks or use creative-only placement, then start the scan.

Scanning is incremental, so large areas take time rather than freezing the server. Keep the area loaded until the used snapshot appears in the output.
