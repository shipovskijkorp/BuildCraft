<chapter name="Copying and Pasting"/>
Use the copier on a configured Gate to store its trigger, action, connection and parameter data. Use the filled copier on another compatible Gate to copy the stored program.

Sneak-right-click in the air to clear the stored data. The item model and tooltip indicate whether a program is currently stored.
