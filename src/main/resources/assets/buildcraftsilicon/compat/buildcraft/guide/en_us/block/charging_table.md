<chapter name="Charging"/>
Place a chargeable item in the single slot. Nearby Lasers supply power to the table, which transfers whole energy units into items exposing the Forge Energy capability.

The table requests only the amount the item can still accept and stops when the item is full. Items that cannot receive energy are rejected.
