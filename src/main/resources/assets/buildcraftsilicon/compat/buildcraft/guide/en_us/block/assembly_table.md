<chapter name="Assembly"/>
Insert the required ingredients and select one or more available assembly recipes. Nearby powered Lasers fill the selected recipe's energy requirement. When complete, the output is placed in the table or offered to adjacent inventories and pipes.

Several selected recipes may be cycled automatically when their ingredients are available. The table stores partial laser progress, so interrupted work can continue later.
