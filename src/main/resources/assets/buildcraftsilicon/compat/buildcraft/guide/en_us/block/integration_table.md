<chapter name="Integration"/>
Place the target item in the centre slot and the required components in the surrounding slots. If a registered integration recipe matches, nearby Lasers provide its required energy and the finished item appears in the output.

A major Robotics use is installing a programmed Redstone Board into an empty Robot. That operation requires 10,000 MJ.
