<chapter name="Programming Boards"/>
Insert a blank Redstone Board, choose a career from the available options and power nearby Lasers. The table outputs a programmed board when the career's energy cost is reached.

Options are ordered by energy cost and name. Basic logistics careers cost 8,000 MJ; common work careers 32,000 MJ; advanced careers 64,000 MJ; and Builder costs 128,000 MJ.
