</no_lore>

<chapter name="Fuel"/>
Heavy Oil burns in a Combustion Engine at 2 MJ/t and produces Residue. It is usable directly, but further refining yields cleaner products.

<chapter name="Refining"/>
Hot Heavy Oil distils into Light Fuel and Dense Oil. Searing Heavy Oil distils into Mixed Heavy Fuel and Residue. Use a Heat Exchanger to select the required temperature before sending it to the Distiller.
