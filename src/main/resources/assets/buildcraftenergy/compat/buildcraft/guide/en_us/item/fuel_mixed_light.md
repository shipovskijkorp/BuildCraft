<lore>
Two useful fuels are sharing one tank. The Distiller can settle the ownership dispute.
</lore>
<no_lore>
Mixed Light Fuel is a clean intermediate and a usable 3 MJ/t Combustion Engine fuel.
</no_lore>

<chapter name="Production"/>
Hot Oil and Hot Distilled Oil produce Mixed Light Fuel as their gaseous output. It contains gaseous and light fuel fractions.

<chapter name="Refining"/>
Distil Cool Mixed Light Fuel to separate Gaseous Fuel and Light Fuel. It may also be burned directly without producing Residue.
