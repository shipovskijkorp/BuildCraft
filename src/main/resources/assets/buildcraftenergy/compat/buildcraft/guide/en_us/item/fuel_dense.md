<lore>
Dense Fuel values endurance over enthusiasm and is quite content to keep an engine working for a long time.
</lore>
<no_lore>
Dense Fuel is a clean refined fuel producing 4 MJ/t with a long burn duration.
</no_lore>
<hint>
<bold>Hint:</bold> Dense Fuel is valuable enough to justify buffered delivery and an engine shutdown trigger before unattended operation.
</hint>


<chapter name="Production"/>
Hot Distilled Oil, Hot Mixed Heavy Fuel and Searing Dense Oil can produce Dense Fuel. The Dense Oil route also separates Residue as its second output.

<chapter name="Power"/>
Dense Fuel burns without creating residue. Its moderate output and high total energy make it well suited to sustained machinery rather than short peak loads.
