A dark liquid bubbling out of the ground is either a valuable energy resource or a very expensive environmental problem.
</lore>
<no_lore>
Oil is the naturally generated starting material for BuildCraft's fuel-processing chain.
</no_lore>

<chapter name="Collecting Oil"/>
Oil deposits generate in the world and can be collected with Buckets, a Pump or other compatible fluid handlers. Store it in Tanks and move it with Fluid Pipes.

<chapter name="Using and Refining"/>
Cool Oil is a dirty Combustion Engine fuel producing 3 MJ/t and Residue. Refining it is normally more valuable.

The Distiller uses the current temperature to choose a route: Cool Oil separates into Gaseous Fuel and Heavy Oil; Hot Oil separates into Mixed Light Fuel and Dense Oil; Searing Oil separates into Distilled Oil and Residue.
