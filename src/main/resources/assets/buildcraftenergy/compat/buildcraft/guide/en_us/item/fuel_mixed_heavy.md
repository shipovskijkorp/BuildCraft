<lore>
Light and dense fuel are compatible tank-mates, but separating them makes their different strengths easier to use.
</lore>
<no_lore>
Mixed Heavy Fuel is a clean intermediate and a usable 5 MJ/t Combustion Engine fuel.
</no_lore>

<chapter name="Production"/>
Cool Distilled Oil and Searing Heavy Oil can produce Mixed Heavy Fuel. It contains light and dense fuel fractions.

<chapter name="Refining"/>
Distil Hot Mixed Heavy Fuel to separate Light Fuel and Dense Fuel. Burning the mixture directly creates no Residue and provides an output between those two final fuels.
