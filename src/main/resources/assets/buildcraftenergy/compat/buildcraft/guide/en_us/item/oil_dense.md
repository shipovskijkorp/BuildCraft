<lore>
Dense Oil contains the part of the original mixture that strongly objects to moving quickly through machinery.
</lore>
<no_lore>
Dense Oil is a dirty intermediate fuel obtained from Hot Oil or Hot Distilled Oil.
</no_lore>

<chapter name="Fuel"/>
Dense Oil burns at 4 MJ/t in a Combustion Engine and produces Residue. Its higher output is useful when immediate power is more important than clean operation.

<chapter name="Refining"/>
Heat Dense Oil to the Searing state and distil it to separate Dense Fuel from Residue. This is the final refining step for its branch of the processing chain.
