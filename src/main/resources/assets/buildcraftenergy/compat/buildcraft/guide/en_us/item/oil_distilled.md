Distilled Oil is a clean-burning intermediate containing gaseous, light and dense fuel fractions.
</no_lore>

<chapter name="Fuel"/>
Distilled Oil burns without residue at 1 MJ/t. Its direct output is modest, but it is a flexible feedstock for further separation.

<chapter name="Refining"/>
Cool Distilled Oil distils into Gaseous Fuel and Mixed Heavy Fuel. Hot Distilled Oil distils into Mixed Light Fuel and Dense Fuel. Change its temperature in a Heat Exchanger before choosing the next route.
