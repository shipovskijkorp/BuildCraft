<no_lore>
A Fragile Fluid Shard is a temporary one-way container holding up to 500 mB of a fluid.
</no_lore>
<hint>
<bold>Hint:</bold> Keep this tool or component near the machines that use it and check the Usages section before crafting large quantities.

<chapter name="Fluid Recovery"/>
Supported blocks may drop these shards when broken with fluid still inside them. Each shard stores up to half a bucket. Fluid handlers can drain the shard, consuming its contents.

The shard cannot be filled manually and is not intended as a reusable tank. Move it into a compatible tank or pipe before treating it as ordinary material.
