<lore>
With many large building projects, flooding large area's with fluids is a tedious task which you could do without.
</lore>
<no_lore>
It is used to automatically place fluids into the world without doing it manually with buckets.
</no_lore>
<hint>
<bold>Hint:</bold> Test a Flood Gate in a contained basin first. Its placement behaviour is much easier to understand before connecting it to a large tank or open landscape.
</hint>

<chapter name="Information"/>
The Floodgate is a power free machine which when fluids are piped into it will place them downwards into the world 
<recipes_usages stack="buildcraftfactory:flood_gate"/>
The floodgate places fluids in a 64 blocks radius from bottom to top and the center outwards.
