<chapter name="Automatic Crafting"/>
Arrange the desired recipe in the phantom 3 by 3 blueprint grid, then supply matching ingredients to the material inventory. The table crafts the result into its output inventory whenever the recipe is valid and enough space is available.

Each operation requests 500 MJ. The table can receive MJ directly and also participates in the laser-table system.
