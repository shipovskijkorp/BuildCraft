The Timer is a pipe pluggable that adds periodic 5, 10 and 15 second Gate triggers.
</no_lore>
<hint>
<bold>Hint:</bold> Configure the pluggable before copying or installing many of them. Test one pipe face first to confirm orientation and gate interaction.

<chapter name="Timer Triggers"/>
Attach the Timer to a pipe. Gates on that pipe gain three internal triggers that pulse every 5, 10 or 15 seconds according to world game time.

The Timer occupies and blocks its side of the pipe like other pluggables.
