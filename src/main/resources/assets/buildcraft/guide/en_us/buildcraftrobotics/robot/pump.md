<chapter name="Career"/>
This career is programmed into a Redstone Board for 32,000 MJ and installed into an empty Robot with an Integration Table.

While active, the robot drains matching world fluid blocks into its internal tank and returns to a station to unload the fluid. It returns to compatible Docking Stations to recharge, load or unload as required.

Use station fluid filters to prevent unrelated fluids from entering the route.
