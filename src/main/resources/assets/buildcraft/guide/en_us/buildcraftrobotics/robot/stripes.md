<chapter name="Career"/>
This career is programmed into a Redstone Board for 64,000 MJ and installed into an empty Robot with an Integration Table.

While active, the robot loads usable items and applies the same item-use handlers as a Stripes Transport Pipe to free target blocks. It returns to compatible Docking Stations to recharge, load or unload as required.
