<chapter name="Genetic Filters"/>
Open the pipe to configure each output face. Forestry filter rules can match genetic items by species, species family and life stage. Up to three selected species can be assigned to a side, and each entry may be enabled or disabled independently.

Items are routed through the first compatible output according to the configured Forestry rules. Outputs without an active matching rule do not claim the genetic item. The pipe can be painted and connected like other BuildCraft item pipes.

This page is loaded only when Forestry is installed and the compatibility item is registered.
