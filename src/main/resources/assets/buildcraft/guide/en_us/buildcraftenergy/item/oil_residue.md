<chapter name="Production"/>
Crude Oil, Heavy Oil and Dense Oil produce Residue while burning in a Combustion Engine. It is also the liquid output of several Searing distillation recipes.

Residue is not registered as a Combustion Engine fuel. Drain it from engines and machines with Fluid Pipes or containers so residue tanks do not become blocked.

<chapter name="Temperature"/>
Like the other BuildCraft oil products, Residue has Cool, Hot and Searing states and can exchange heat in a Heat Exchanger. It is primarily a by-product and transportable process fluid in the current project.
