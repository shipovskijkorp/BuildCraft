<chapter name="Career"/>
This career is programmed into a Redstone Board for 64,000 MJ and installed into an empty Robot with an Integration Table.

While active, the robot loads TNT, flies over valid ground in its work zone and drops primed explosives. It returns to compatible Docking Stations to recharge, load or unload as required.
