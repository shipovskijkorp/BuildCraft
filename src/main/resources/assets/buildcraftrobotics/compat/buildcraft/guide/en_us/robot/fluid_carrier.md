<chapter name="Career"/>
This career is programmed into a Redstone Board for 8,000 MJ and installed into an empty Robot with an Integration Table.

While active, the robot moves fluids between stations and tanks, using station fluid filters to choose what may be loaded or unloaded. It returns to compatible Docking Stations to recharge, load or unload as required.

Use station fluid filters to prevent unrelated fluids from entering the route.
