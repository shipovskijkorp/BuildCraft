<chapter name="Career"/>
This career is programmed into a Redstone Board for 32,000 MJ and installed into an empty Robot with an Integration Table.

While active, the robot finds mature crops in its work zone, harvests them and returns the drops to a station. It returns to compatible Docking Stations to recharge, load or unload as required.
