<chapter name="Career"/>
This career is programmed into a Redstone Board for 128,000 MJ and installed into an empty Robot with an Integration Table.

While active, the robot finds nearby Builders or Construction Markers, reserves construction tasks, fetches materials and performs the work. It returns to compatible Docking Stations to recharge, load or unload as required.

Builder Robots can work for both stationary Builders and Construction Markers. Keep the project and supply stations inside the robot's reachable zone.
