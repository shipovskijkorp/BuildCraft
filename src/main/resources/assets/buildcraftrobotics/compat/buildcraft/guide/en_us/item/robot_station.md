<lore>
Even the most independent robot needs somewhere to charge, sleep and be told what not to pick up.
</lore>
<no_lore>
A Docking Station is a pipe pluggable that links, charges and controls Robots.
</no_lore>

<recipes_usages stack="buildcraftrobotics:robot_station"/>

<chapter name="Placement"/>
Place the station on any free side of a pipe. Use a programmed Robot on the station to deploy it. A station can be occupied by only one robot at a time.

A pipe network carrying MJ can recharge the docked robot. Sneak-use a Wrench on a linked main station to release its robot back into item form when supported by the station state.

<chapter name="Gate Control"/>
Attach a Gate to the pipe and use Robotics station actions to accept or provide items and fluids, request resources, forbid or force robots, and expose machine requests. Map Locations can restrict work or loading zones.
