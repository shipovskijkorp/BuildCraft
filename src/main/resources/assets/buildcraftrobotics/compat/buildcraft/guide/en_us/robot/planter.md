<chapter name="Career"/>
This career is programmed into a Redstone Board for 32,000 MJ and installed into an empty Robot with an Integration Table.

While active, the robot loads matching seeds and plants them on valid soil positions in its work zone. It returns to compatible Docking Stations to recharge, load or unload as required.

Use Gate filters and Lists on the linked stations to restrict which items are handled.
