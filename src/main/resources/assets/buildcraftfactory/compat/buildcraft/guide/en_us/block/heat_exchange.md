<chapter name="Building the Multiblock"/>
A Heat Exchanger consists of a start, one or more middle sections and an end connected in a straight line. Up to three middle sections can be used; additional valid sections increase the amount processed by the assembled machine.

<chapter name="Using Heat"/>
Feed a heatable fluid into the heating side and a coolable fluid into the cooling side. Heat is transferred rather than created for free: one stream becomes hotter while the other becomes cooler. BuildCraft oil products move through Cool, Hot and Searing states in ten millibucket recipe units.

Water can absorb heat, while Lava can supply it. Fluid direction and available output space determine whether the exchanger can continue processing.

The selected temperature matters to the Distiller, so route fluids through the Heat Exchanger before the next refining stage when necessary.
