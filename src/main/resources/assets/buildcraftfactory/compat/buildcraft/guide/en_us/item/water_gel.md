<chapter name="Using the Gelifier"/>
Use the item while pointing at water. The first gel block is placed at the targeted water position and begins spreading through neighbouring source water and supported aquatic plants.

Water Gel advances through several stages and eventually solidifies. Contact with surrounding water slows the final solidification, allowing the gel front to spread before it becomes stable.

<chapter name="Recovering Gel"/>
Breaking solidified Water Gel drops Gelled Water items. The process is intended for controlled water removal and temporary construction, so test it on a small pool before applying it to an ocean.
